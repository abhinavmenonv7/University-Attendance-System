University Attendance System

Problem Statement:

Manual attendance tracking in universities is time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult to audit. Faculty spend valuable class time calling roll or passing around sheets, records are often lost or inconsistently maintained, and students have no easy way to verify their own attendance percentage before it affects exam eligibility. There is a need for a centralized, digital system that records, manages, and reports student attendance accurately and efficiently.

Scope of the Project:

The system will:
- Allow faculty to mark attendance for their assigned classes (daily/period-wise).
- Allow students to view their attendance records and overall percentage.
- Allow administrators to manage courses, faculty, students, and class schedules.
- Generate attendance reports (per student, per course, per date range).
- Send alerts/notifications when a student's attendance falls below a defined threshold (e.g., 75%).

Out of scope (for initial version):

- Biometric or facial recognition-based attendance capture.
- Integration with external university ERP systems.
- Mobile app (initial version will be web-based only).

Target Users:

- Students – view their own attendance records and percentages.
- Faculty/Teachers – mark and update attendance for their classes.
- Administrators – manage user accounts, courses, and generate institution-wide reports.
- HOD/Coordinators (optional) – monitor department-level attendance trends.

High-Level Features:

1. User Authentication & Role Management – separate login/access for students, faculty, and admins.
2. Attendance Marking – faculty can mark present/absent for each class session.
3. Attendance Dashboard – students and faculty view attendance summaries and trends.
4. Course & Timetable Management – admin sets up courses, sections, and class schedules.
5. Reports & Analytics – exportable attendance reports (CSV/PDF) by student, course, or date range.
6. Low-Attendance Alerts – automatic notifications to students/faculty when attendance drops below threshold.
7. Search & Filter – quickly locate a student's or course's attendance history.
